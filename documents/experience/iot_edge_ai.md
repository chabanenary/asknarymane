# IoT & Edge AI Experience

## Embedded Systems as an IoT Foundation
Narymane's entire career has been built around what is now called IoT and edge infrastructure: developing the software running on network equipment deployed by the thousands at the edge of telecom networks (13 years at OneAccess / Ekinops), then, since June 2026, the software and AI of an embedded conversational device on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.

## Hardware Experience
- NXP/Freescale PowerPC and QorIQ processors (P1021, T1040, PowerPC 83xx), MIPS, ARM, embedded and virtualized x86
- Devices with Ethernet, Wi-Fi, 4G/LTE, xDSL and optical SFP interfaces
- NVIDIA Jetson Nano and Jetson Orin Nano for GPU-accelerated edge inference
- Comfortable reading electrical schematics and datasheets to write drivers
- End-to-end board bring-up: from JTAG programming to system validation
- Integration of peripherals on Jetson: camera, microphone array, touchscreen, Bluetooth audio

## Operating Systems
- Linux: Debian, Ubuntu, Yocto/OpenEmbedded (kernel configuration, patches, device tree, custom images), JetPack 6 / L4T
- VxWorks RTOS: real-time multitasking, interrupt handling, IPC
- U-Boot, firmware update mechanisms, secure boot
- systemd, udev, NetworkManager, initialization sequences for constrained boot environments

## Networking & Protocols
- Deep experience: TCP/IP, xDSL (ADSL/VDSL), ATM/QoS, Ethernet switching, Wi-Fi, 4G/LTE
- DHCP, DNS, HTTP/REST, RTSP, SIP, Socket.IO / WebSocket
- Knowledge of MQTT
- Multi-service devices handling data, voice and video simultaneously

## Edge AI (since 2024)
- YOLO model training with Ultralytics / PyTorch
- Model optimization chain: PyTorch → ONNX → TensorRT (Jetson), TFLite / LiteRT (Android), llama.cpp and 4-bit quantization (Jetson Orin Nano)
- Real-time video pipelines: GStreamer, RTSP, OpenCV
- Deployment under strict memory, GPU, temperature and power constraints
- On-device inference on Android with TFLite / LiteRT and GPU delegate
- Real-time voice and vision on Jetson Orin Nano, with under one second of latency
