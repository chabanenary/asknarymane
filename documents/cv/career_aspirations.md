## Key Strengths — Reliability in Real-World Conditions
The common thread of her whole career is the reliability of systems in production: specify, measure, test, monitor, then close the loop between observed failures and tests. At OneAccess / Ekinops, she drove the defect loop end to end (trouble reports created automatically from test failures, tracked fixes, tests reinstated in non-regression). On her embedded AI mission, she applies the same discipline: 30-minute and 2-hour endurance tests without any crash, resource monitoring, graceful degradation. On GalleryKeeper, she audited the model's evaluation before touching the model itself.

## Key Strengths — Systems-Level Thinking
Her most striking ability is keeping the whole system in mind at once — from the lowest-level hardware constraints up to the highest-level application behavior. In carrier-grade telecom, where a firmware bug can mean interventions on thousands of deployed devices, this systems-level view is critical.

## Key Strengths — Code Quality and Engineering Discipline
She writes code designed to be reused, debugged and maintained by others. Documentation, meaningful naming, explicit error handling and rigorous code review are non-negotiable practices. She worked for 13 years within a CMMI Level 3 quality framework.

## Key Strengths — Fast Learner
Every major skill beyond her initial education — Yocto, U-Boot, Python, Android, YOLO model training, FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, LLMs and RAG, real-time embedded AI — was self-taught, through determination and practice, and turned into published projects.

## Key Strengths — Real-Time Systems
Deep understanding of real-time constraints: priority-based scheduling, interrupt latency, mutex and semaphore management, deadlock avoidance, shared-memory synchronization. This background shapes her whole approach to software design: she reasons in terms of determinism, resource contention and failure modes — now applied to real-time voice and vision pipelines.

## Key Strengths — Multi-Platform Deployment
She has deployed the same vision model on very different environments: mobile (Android / TFLite), GPU edge device (Jetson Nano / TensorRT) and server or cloud (FastAPI / ONNX Runtime / Docker / Kubernetes / OpenShift on AWS). This is the complete skill set of an ML deployment engineer.

## Key Strengths — Communication and Documentation
Years of coordinating offshore teams and writing specifications, test plans and design documents have made documentation and clarity a reflex. She writes technical reports and comparative summaries for decision-makers, and publishes technical articles on Dev.to.
